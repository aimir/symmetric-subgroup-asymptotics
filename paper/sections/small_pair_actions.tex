\subsection{Three pairs and the complete degree-sixteen slice}

\begin{proposition}[Three original pairs]\label{prop:three-pair-actions}
Every nonbinary transitive action on three pairs has a complete
contractive forward row and negligible scalar.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The full pair wreath group has the decomposition
\[
 C_2\wr S_3\cong C_2\times(A\rtimes S_3)
 \cong C_2\times S_4,
\]
where $\F_2^3=C\oplus A$ is the invariant sum of the constant line
and the augmentation plane. The affine action of $A\rtimes S_3$
on the four vectors of $A$ is faithful and has order $24$.
Let $H$ be the image of the original action in this product, and let
$L\leq S_4$ be its first projection. Transitivity gives $6\mid|H|$,
hence $3\mid|L|$. Thus $|L|$ is $3,6,12$ or $24$.
An order-six subgroup has a normal cubic subgroup, whose unique fixed
point is fixed by the whole subgroup; restriction to the other three
points identifies it with $S_3$. The order-twelve subgroup is $A_4$,
and the order-twenty-four subgroup is $S_4$.

The kernel of $H\to L$ is either trivial or the central $C_2$.
In the first case $H\cong L$; in the second $H=L\times C_2$.
The order-three case is not transitive on six points. Consequently every
transitive actual group is isomorphic to one of
\[
 C_6,\quad S_3,\quad A_4,\quad S_4,\quad
 C_2\times S_3,\quad C_2\times A_4,\quad C_2\times S_4.
\]
These are abstract charts on the original groups. Distinct permutation
actions, including the two $S_4$ actions, retain their original pointings.

The targets $C_6,A_4,S_3,C_2\times S_3,S_4$ have specified
faithful representations of degrees at most five, less than six.
Use the target itself in Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-comparator},
with $C=0$. For $U=C_2\times L$, $L=A_4$ or $S_4$, let
$V$ be the standard $V_4$. Any normal subgroup not containing
$V$ centralizes it; normality then forces that subgroup into the
central $C_2$. Normals containing $V$ contribute exactly
$Z_J(C_2\times(L/V))$, whose comparator has faithful degree five.
The two other quotients are $L$ and $C_2\times L$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:central-repeat}, with repeated rank one
and central rank at most one, bounds both their onto-map counts
by a fixed constant times $2^{b/2}$. Thus
\[
 Z_J(U)\leq Z_J(C_2\times(L/V))+C_U2^{b/2}.
\]
Its exponent $1/2<6/8$ is strict. The finite comparator theorem
applies with the original normalizer for each original action.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every nonbinary action of degree sixteen]
\label{thm:degree-sixteen-nonbinary}
The complete union of nonbinary transitive degree-sixteen actions
has a contractive forward row and negligible scalar. In particular
this covers every eight-pair action and every zero-ternary action
on four four-point blocks.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The finite action certificate contains the full nonbinary slice of
the degree-sixteen transitive classification, with 527 original
permutation representatives and their original normalizers. Its
completeness premise is the stated transitive classification
\cite{Hulpke2005,TransGrp365}. Each of its 9249 literal normal
entries is retained separately. To justify normal completeness,
the certificate lists all conjugacy classes, their exact sizes
and normal closures. The supplied normals contain one and are
closed under adjoining every such normal closure. Every normal
subgroup is generated by the closures of the classes it contains,
so this join closure proves that no original normal is omitted.

For 518 actions all quotients have a fixed mixed character tuple,
and for all but a specified interval or head quotient of each
remaining action the same is true. A mixed tuple has $l$ linear
and $t$ general slots with literal kernel intersection equal to
the original normal, and satisfies
\[
 3^{64l}5^{64t}\leq2^{381}.
\]
Proposition~\ref{prop:character-count} gives coefficient at most
$381/192=2-1/64$, strictly below the physical reserve $16/8$.
All target automorphism factors are fixed finite constants.

Two actions have a specified original derived subgroup with
quotient $C_2^4$, concretely represented on eight points. Precisely
the normal interval above that subgroup is retained as the whole
$Z_J(C_2^4)$; every other original normal has a mixed tuple.
Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-comparator} applies with $v=8$ and
$\eta=2-1/64$.

Seven actions have, among their original normals, eight exceptional
quotients. Each is a central elementary binary extension of a
repeated $A_4$ or $S_4$ model: the certified whole centre has
rank $c$, and the quotient has repeated rank $d$, with
$32\max(c,d)\leq127$. The maps and central preimages are literal;
no splitting is inferred. Proposition~\ref{prop:central-repeat}
bounds each onto fibre by a fixed constant times
$2^{\max(c,d)b/2}\leq2^{3b/2}$. All other normals are mixed.
Original-weight pointing and Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-delta} give
their direct exponential row. The finite union, with the two
comparator scalars, proves the theorem. No certificate is selected
after inspecting the complete source $J$.
\end{proof}
