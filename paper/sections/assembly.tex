\section{Exhaustive counting and boundedness-first assembly}
\label{sec:assembly}
Let $F_N$ be the normalized count of all fixed-point-free binary
subgroups of $S_{2N}$, and retain the complete binary error $E_N$
from Section~\ref{sec:markers}. Actual orbit projections give the
exact disjoint partition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-partition}
 F_N=C_{2N}^{\crit}+E_N.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Ordinary-target binary frontier transport]
\label{prop:binary-frontier-transport}
Put
\[
 B_{2N}=E_N,\qquad B_{2N+1}=u_NE_N+v_NE_{N-1}.
\]
There are nonnegative functions $\eta_B^{\rm amb}(n)$ and
$K_B^{\rm amb}(n,m)$, supported on $m<n$, and constants
$a_B',C_B'>0$ such that eventually
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-ambient-recurrence}
 B_n\leq\eta_B^{\rm amb}(n)
       +\sum_{m<n}K_B^{\rm amb}(n,m)A_m,
 \qquad
 \eta_B^{\rm amb}(n)+\sum_{m<n}K_B^{\rm amb}(n,m)
       \leq C_B'2^{-a_B'n}.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:binary-recurrence} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-native-recurrence}
 E_N\leq\eta_B(N)+\sum_{m<2N}K_B(N,m)A_m,
 \qquad\eta_B(N)+\sum_{m<2N}K_B(N,m)\leq C_B2^{-a_BN}.
\end{equation}
In even ambient degree use \eqref{eq:binary-native-recurrence} directly.
In odd degree apply the same recurrence
at $N$ and $N-1$, multiply the two inequalities by $u_N$ and $v_N$,
and pad both target rows by zero to the full range $m<2N+1$.  The coefficients are
nonnegative, and Section~\ref{sec:markers} gives
\[
 u_N\leq4(N+1),\qquad v_N\leq2\Phi^{-2}(N+1)^3.
\]
These fixed polynomial factors are absorbed by any smaller positive
exponential rate. Since $n=2N$ or $2N+1$, this proves
\eqref{eq:binary-ambient-recurrence}.  Thus the binary recurrence is
transported before any boundedness of $F$, $E$, or $A$ is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exhaustive ordinary counting estimate]
\label{thm:global-counting}
Write $R_n=A_n-C_n^{\crit}$ for the normalized complement of the
complete critical family. There are nonnegative functions $\eta(n)$
and $\mathcal K(n,m)$, supported on $m<n$, and constants $a,C>0$
such that eventually
\begin{equation}\label{eq:master}
 0\leq R_n\leq\eta(n)+\sum_{m<n}\mathcal K(n,m)A_m,
 \qquad
 \eta(n)+\sum_{m<n}\mathcal K(n,m)\leq C2^{-an}.
\end{equation}
The kernel is the sum of the outside-action row, the exact positive
marker row, and the transported binary row of
Proposition~\ref{prop:binary-frontier-transport}. Every forward edge
has one complete ordinary target.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First take all subgroups with an actual transitive orbit outside
the alphabet consisting of binary groups, natural $C_3,A_4,S_3$,
and fixed points. Theorem~\ref{thm:nonbinary-alphabet} bounds this
complete family by an exponentially small ordinary forward row
and a quadratically small scalar. Every pointing deletes its whole
original orbit, so its exact weight is
$n!/(b!a(U))$. A cold estimate uses it in the form
$\Delta(n,b)/a(U)$ on $A_b$; a hot estimate retains the factor
$1/(b!a(U)p_nG_{\lfloor n/2\rfloor})$ on its complete source sum.
These are bounds for already assembled subgroups, including their
other orbits and any external characters.
In the pair-action rows the faithful top is used only as a cover of the
actual quotient $B_N=U/(KN)$; the counted extension remains
$U/N\twoheadrightarrow B_N$. Thus this certificate choice introduces no
new orbit occurrence or normalizer factor.

In the complementary alphabet let $c,t$ be the numbers of natural
$C_3,A_4$ orbits. If $t\geq n/1000$, apply the complete scalar
of Theorem~\ref{thm:macro-a4}. If $t<n/1000$ and $c+t>0$, apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:low-a4-transfer}. Its intrinsic action condition
holds here: every relative ternary head of a two-group is zero;
the normals $1,C_3,S_3$ of natural $S_3$ also have zero relative
ternary head, since $[C_3,S_3]=C_3$ and $S_3$ has no ternary
abelian quotient. Fixed points contribute zero. This proves the
condition for every residual action in the alphabet, without an
assumption about which normal kernel is selected later. Add the
ordinary row and scalar of this theorem once. The macro and small
packet sectors are disjoint by their original-degree cutoff.

It remains that every moved orbit is binary or natural $S_3$.
Write the marker counts as $g,f$, and $n=2N+\eps$. Under the
exact collapse of Proposition~\ref{prop:complete-marker-collapse},
let $q$ be the number of distinct marker signs and put
$D=(g+f-\eps)/2$. This is an integer, because the binary exterior
has even degree, and is nonnegative. When $D\geq1$, the complete
row in Theorem~\ref{thm:complete-marker-row} applies directly with
the collection of all collapsed subgroups.  It contributes
$\sum_{m<n}K_{\rm mark}(n,m)A_m$, and its row is exponentially
small without any boundedness assumption on $A_m$.

When $D=0$, in even degree there are no markers or fixed points.
The complete binary partition gives $C_{2N}^{\crit}+E_N$, each
once. In odd degree there is exactly one fixed point or one
natural $S_3$ orbit. With critical binary orbits these are exactly
the two disjoint odd parts of $C_n^{\crit}$. Otherwise their exact
contributions are $\alpha_NE_N$ and $\beta_NpE_N$; the complete
pair moment of Proposition~\ref{prop:binary-moment} bounds their
sum by $u_NE_N+v_NE_{N-1}$. Thus the entire zero-defect remainder
is $B_n$. Proposition~\ref{prop:binary-frontier-transport} replaces
this term by one exponentially small scalar and one strictly forward
ordinary row. No estimate for a restricted binary residual is
substituted for the complete moment.

These sectors exhaust all actual subgroups and are pairwise
disjoint before their positive enlargements. Within the first
sector fix an order of the proved action families and assign a
subgroup to the first one it meets, before testing temperatures.
For each assigned family its hot part means that some accepted
pointing is hot, and its cold part that none is hot. Each theorem's
complete positive envelope is then paid once, over all its
assigned inputs. Multiple orbit occurrences are already accounted
for by pointing the original weight
$\prod_U(a(U)^{m_U}m_U!)^{-1}$; auxiliary representations and
alternative certificates contribute no new physical factorial.
All subsequent sectors are restrictions of their proved positive
envelopes and are likewise paid once.

Define $\eta$ to be the sum of the nonbinary, macro, small-packet,
and transported binary scalars. Define $\mathcal K$ to be the sum
of the nonbinary and small-packet ordinary rows, the positive marker
row, and the transported binary row. Each summand is nonnegative,
strictly forward, and exponentially small in total mass. Taking the
minimum of their positive rates gives \eqref{eq:master}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
The critical ratios $C_n^{\crit}$ are bounded. Choose an onset at
which the row in \eqref{eq:master} is at most $1/2$, and let $M$
exceed all finitely many initial $A_m$ and twice a uniform bound for
$C_n^{\crit}+\eta(n)$.  The exact partition
$A_n=C_n^{\crit}+R_n$ and induction in $n$ give $A_n\leq M$ for
every $n$. Substituting this established bound into
\eqref{eq:master} gives $R_n=O(2^{-cn})$ for some $c>0$.
The critical-family estimate now yields
$|A_n-1|=O(2^{-c'n})$. Taking the smaller positive rate proves the
result at both parities. No boundedness premise was used in deriving
any row or scalar; boundedness is a consequence of their eventual
contraction.
\end{proof}
