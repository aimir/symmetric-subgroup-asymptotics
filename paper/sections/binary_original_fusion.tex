\section{Direct fusion for original binary orbits}
\label{sec:binary-original-fusion}
The following cut-or-character alternative gives a shorter route to
original local entries from width $32$. It uses the deterministic
separator and the actual action kernel, without the rank-moment
calculation or the adaptive degree-$32$ cut. Its recurrence has complete
ordinary subgroup counts at smaller degrees as targets. Thus it can be
inserted directly in the ordinary recurrence. The complete binary-error
recurrence of the preceding section also ends in complete ordinary targets;
the theorem below is a parallel ambient estimate for sources having a large
binary orbit. It may replace the corresponding large-orbit component when
used, but it introduces neither a second target sequence nor an independent
boundedness induction.

\subsection{An original cut-or-character alternative}

Write $B_j=\binom{j}{\lfloor j/2\rfloor}$ and
$\gamma=5^{2/7}$. The permutation class estimate used in Proposition~\ref{prop:anchor-count}
implies the weaker bound $k(P)^7\leq5^{2b}$ for every actual binary
subgroup $P\leq S_b$.
Consequently, for a finite binary group $Q$, with
$z=\dim\Omega_1Z(Q)$, the original character envelope is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-seven-original-envelope}
 |\operatorname{Epi}(J,Q)|
 \leq |\operatorname{Aut}Q|\,2^{\alpha(z)b},
 \qquad \alpha(z)=\frac{2z}{7}\log_2 5,
 \qquad J\leq S_b.
\end{equation}
Here $J$ is arbitrary: restriction to its original Sylow $2$-subgroup
is injective on epimorphisms to $Q$ and remains surjective onto $Q$.
The same bound holds for any specified surviving subfamily by inclusion.
This is the separate $\gamma$ envelope, not a replacement of every
previously accepted $38/25$ criterion.

\begin{theorem}[Original cut-or-character alternative]
\label{thm:binary-original-cut-or-character}
Let $U\leq S_{2h}$ be transitive and binary, where $h=2^k$ and
$k\geq4$. Fix an original invariant pair frame, with flip kernel $K$
and faithful transitive top $T\leq S_h$. For each original
$N\lhd U$, retain
\[
 Q=U/N,\qquad A=K/(K\cap N),\qquad B=U/(KN),
\]
including the actual map $T\twoheadrightarrow B$ and the actual
kernel chart $A\longrightarrow Q\twoheadrightarrow B$.
At least one of the following alternatives holds.
\begin{enumerate}
\item There is a literal subspace $C\leq A^B$ such that
\[
 2\dim C+4\dim(A/C)^B\leq h-2.
\]
\item The action of $T$ on $A$ is faithful, the whole center of $Q$
lies in $A$, and
\[
 \Omega_1Z(Q)=A^B,\qquad
 z=\dim\Omega_1Z(Q)\leq B_k,\qquad
 5^{16z}<2^{7(2h)}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
In particular each original $N$ has a same-source fusion entry with
an even prefix $v<2h$ and a strictly positive gap. No splitting of
$Q\to B$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $S=A^B$, $t=\dim S$, $\ell=\dim(A/S)^B$, and
$D_0=2B_{k-1}$. These are dimensions of the same actual section, so
$t+\ell\leq\dim A$. The original permutation-section bound and its
one-character kernel restriction give
$t\leq B_k\leq D_0$ and the complete displacement line-slice bound
$D_0-t$. The deterministic separator supplies $q$ independent
functionals on $S$, with annihilator $C$, such that
\[
 \dim C=t-q,\qquad \dim(A/C)^B=q,\qquad
 2q\leq\ell+\max\{1,D_0-t\}.
\]
The fixed-quotient identity uses the full displacement slice; it
does not merely test each scalar line separately.

If $2\dim A\leq h$, the resulting even cost satisfies
\[
 2\dim C+4\dim(A/C)^B=2t+2q
 \leq t+\ell+D_0+1.
\]
The inequalities $8D_0\leq3h$ and $h\geq16$ make the last expression
at most $h-1$. Evenness gives the first alternative. The inequality
$t\leq D_0$ is retained here; truncating a negative difference would
not justify the displayed estimate.

Suppose instead that $2\dim A>h$. Let $H$ be the actual normal kernel
of $T$ acting on $A$. If $H\ne1$, its orbits on the original $h$
points have a common size $2^a\geq2$: normality gives uniformity,
and faithfulness of $T$ excludes singleton orbits. The original
permutation preimage of $A$, restricted to $H$, maps onto the trivial
$H$-module $A$. Applying the orbit-section bound and
$2B_a\leq2^a$ therefore gives $2\dim A\leq h$, a contradiction.
Thus $T$ acts faithfully on $A$. Since this action factors through
the actual surjection $T\to B$, that map is injective as well.

For any central element of the original $Q$, its image in $B$ acts
trivially on the literal kernel $A$. Faithfulness places the element
in $A$. The actual kernel chart now identifies $Z(Q)$ with $A^B$;
in particular this center is elementary. Finally $8B_k\leq3h$ gives
$16z\leq3(2h)$, and $125<128$ gives the strict integer test.
\end{proof}

For the first alternative put $c=\dim C$, $r=\dim(A/C)^B$ and choose
the entry
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-cut-entry-original}
 v=h+2c,\quad \lambda=r/2,\quad
 D_C=|A/C|\,|H^1(B,A/C)|,\quad
 \Phi_C(J)=|\operatorname{Epi}(J,B)|\,2^{c d_2(J)}.
\end{equation}
The original central extension and its actual quotient by $C$ give
the envelope $D_C2^{\lambda b}\Phi_C(J)$. Its full cocycle factor is
retained. The faithful original cover $T\leq S_h$ mapping onto the
same $B$ gives all common-source moments
\[
 \sum_{J\leq S_b}\Phi_C(J)^q\leq s_{b+qv}
 \qquad(q\geq1).
\]
There is no assertion that $B$ itself has a faithful action of degree
$h$. In the character alternative take
\[
 v=0,\quad\lambda=\alpha(z),\quad
 D_C=|\operatorname{Aut}Q|,\quad\Phi_C(J)=1.
\]
Its moments equal $s_b$. In both alternatives define
\[
 e=\frac{2h-v-8\lambda}{16}>0.
\]
The cut alternative has $2h-v-8\lambda\geq2$, but no such uniform
lower bound is asserted for the character alternative. For each fixed
finite collection of widths, positivity supplies a finite-menu minimum.
All entries are chosen before the exterior degree and the exterior
subgroup; the original normalizer is unchanged.

\subsection{The ordinary forward recurrence for binary orbits}

Let $s_n$ denote the number of all subgroups of $S_n$, let
$L_n>0$ be the exact parity-dependent benchmark, and put
$\sigma_n=s_n/L_n$. Let $\mathcal A_n$ be the family of
literal $H\leq S_n$ having an actual orbit of size at least $32$ whose
restriction image is binary. The entire $H$, its complementary
restriction, and their correlation are unrestricted.

\begin{theorem}[Original ordinary binary-orbit recurrence]
\label{thm:binary-original-ordinary-recurrence}
There is a nonnegative row $K(n,m)$, with $K(n,m)=0$ when $m\geq n$,
such that for $n\geq2048$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-original-ordinary-recurrence}
 \frac{|\mathcal A_n|}{L_n}
 \leq\sum_{m<n}K(n,m)\sigma_m.
\end{equation}
There are constants $C,\kappa>0$ for which
\[
 \sum_{m<n}K(n,m)\leq C2^{-\kappa n}
\]
for all sufficiently large $n$. In particular the row sum is eventually
at most $1/2$. Any subfamily defined by additional conditions on the
original $H$ obeys the same recurrence by inclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An actual binary transitive orbit has power-of-two degree. For each
width $w=2h\in\{32,64,128,256,512\}$, choose one representative of
each original action-conjugacy class and one invariant pairing for
each representative. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:binary-original-cut-or-character}
to every literal original normal subgroup. Relabelling the actual
orbit and its actual complement gives physical coverage by these
families. This is an upper union of original subgroups; no uniqueness
of the chosen orbit or of its chart is required.

For a fixed representative $U$, normal axis $N$ and exterior degree
$b=n-w$, use only the first same-source moment. Its exact contribution
at the target degree $m=b+v$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:binary-original-direct-coefficient}
 \frac{n!}{b!\,|N_{S_w}(U)|}\,
 D_C2^{\lambda b}\,
 \frac{L_{b+v}}{L_n}.
\end{equation}
Every original normal is retained, including distinct summands with
the same target degree. Since $v<w$, every such target is smaller than
$n$. The exact benchmark ratio and $e>0$ give exponential decay for
this genuinely fixed finite menu, without an a priori estimate on
the sequence $s_m$.

The widths $1024$ and $2048$ use their already proved positive zero-cut
entries and the same finite first-moment argument. For widths at least
 $4096$ use the original wide zero-cut installation and its proved
normalizer-weighted menu aggregate. The actual zero entries are bounded
by that full nonnegative row; numerical acceptance of extra entries
is not used as a substitute for a local envelope. Its row sum has the
proved uniform exponential bound.

Add these rows and cover the literal family $\mathcal A_n$ by the
finite-width and wide sectors. Overlap only enlarges the upper bound.
Adding their exponential estimates gives the asserted $C,\kappa$ and
eventual contraction. All denominators in
\eqref{eq:binary-original-direct-coefficient} remain the original
symmetric normalizers.
\end{proof}
