\large The critical model, section capacity, and global reduction}

\begin{abstract}
Let $s_n$ be the number of actual subgroups of $S_n$, and let
$G_r$ count the subspaces of $\F_2^r$. We prove
\[
 s_n=n!G_{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}[y^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}]
 (1+y/6)^{n\bmod2}\exp(y/2+y^2/6+y^4/384)
 \bigl(1+O(2^{-cn})\bigr)
\]
for a constant $c>0$, common to both parities. The critical family includes
all central lifts and the two odd-degree markers. The upper bound uses an
annihilator-aware section capacity theorem, joint-source quotient moments,
and complete weighted recurrences that establish boundedness before using
it. We prove the necessary orbit-action exhaustion and give explicit
finite certificates for its bounded cases. The split $c=1$ case is a
separate application and a check on the retained joint data. A saddle-point
formula has relative error $O(n^{-1})$; an elementary expression has four
residue-class constants and a first correction of order $n^{-1/4}$.
\end{abstract}
