# Constructive six-pair certificate

The compressed JSON [data](../data/six_block.json.gz) cover every faithful
transitive action on twelve points with a system of six two-point blocks
and local component C2. A top has degree six; the original action and its
normalizer always have degree twelve. Quotient models carry no physical
orbit weight.

## Action coverage

The `subgroups` list contains 56 literal subgroups of S6. At each subgroup H,
`edges` retain an adjoining element x, a target index and an actual
conjugating permutation from <H,x> to that target. Replay verifies that the
double cosets HxH represented by those edges cover all 720 elements of S6.
The list contains the trivial subgroup and is pairwise nonconjugate.

For x=h1*g*h2 with h1,h2 in H, <H,x>=<H,g>. Consequently every possible
adjoining step is represented. Induction on a generating sequence proves
coverage of every subgroup of S6. Exactly sixteen supplied classes are
transitive. Neither a catalogue lookup nor an observed catalogue length
enters this argument.

The natural pair kernel M=C2^6 has 2,825 subspaces, since

    sum_(k=0)^6 GaussianBinomial(6,k;2)
      =1+63+651+1395+651+63+1=2825.

`subspaces` lists that many distinct literal subgroups of M. Testing every
one for invariance under every transitive top yields exactly 112 interfaces.
For each (T,E), let W=M:T. The `complements` are literal preimages U of
complements to M/E in W/E, including intransitive preimages.

Replay computes an exact presentation of T on its declared generators,
checks its relators on those generators and its order, and evaluates each
relator after assigning one binary basis vector to one generator lift.
These evaluations give the linear equations for Z1(T,M/E). The recorded
`dimensions` are

    [dim(M/E), dim Z1, dim (M/E)^T, dim H1].

The supplied complements are full, pairwise nonconjugate and number exactly
2^dimH1. Conjugacy here is equivalent to conjugacy by the abelian base:
in W/E=(M/E)H, the component in H fixes H and can be moved past a base
conjugator. Thus the independent cocycle count proves completeness of the
graph list. The original extension U over E need not split.

Each transitive preimage retains an `action` index and a degree-twelve
conjugator. There are 224 block certificates and 182 pairwise nonconjugate
physical actions. Every physical action is reached, and its entire original
symmetric normalizer is checked by equality. Multiple block systems do not
create extra action weights.

## Complete original normal lists

Each physical action contains all its literal `normals`. The additional
`class_representatives` are checked to lie in distinct conjugacy classes;
the sum of their class sizes equals the group order. For each representative
x form its normal closure A=<x>^U. Deduplicate these closures in first-occurrence
order. Each normal record's `joins` supplies the index of NA for every A.
Replay checks every join and requires the identity normal.

Every normal subgroup is generated by the conjugacy classes it contains.
Closure from the identity under these normal joins therefore proves that the
supplied normal list is exhaustive. Replay never calls `NormalSubgroups`.
There are 2,476 literal normal records on the 182 physical actions.

## Quotient certificates

`character_kernels` contains the distinct pairs `[normal_index,degree]`
realized by irreducible characters of the original U. The checker computes
Irr(U) and compares this complete set. A normal record has one of two types:

* `characters`: an empty list only when N=U, one irreducible, or two
  irreducibles with the first explicitly linear. Their actual kernel
  intersection must be N. Thus the characters factor through the same U/N
  and jointly form a faithful cover there.
* `target`: an index into the five fixed models below and one image per
  original U-generator. The actual map must be onto with exact kernel N.

The five models are C2^3, B32, Q0, C2 x Q0 and C2^2 x Q0. On two four-point
blocks set V=V4 x V4. Then

    B32=<V,(23)(67)>,      Q0=<V,(123)(567)>.

These have orders 32 and 48 respectively. Q0 retains the same scalar C3 on
both F4 summands. Literal maps, rather than abstract labels, bind each
exceptional quotient to its model. Original normals with isomorphic
quotients remain separate records.

All permutations are one-based image lists of their stated degree. All
indices are one-based. The footer `counts` object is recomputed. The data
schema is version1.

## Mathematical boundary

The finite checks establish action coverage, all-normal coverage, actual
normalizers and the stated quotient alternatives. The same-source character
inflation bound, the canonical binary quotient generator bound and the
joint repeated-F4 epimorphism theorem supply the separate counting estimates.
In particular the repeated-F4 target does not receive two independent source
copies. Hot/cold aggregation, original action weights and the single terminal
attachment are parts of the mathematical argument.

GAP's group, character, presentation and normalizer algorithms, together with
the supplied code and parser, remain computational trust inputs. This is not
formal verification. Execution is documented in
[SIX_BLOCK.md](../../computations/gap/SIX_BLOCK.md).
