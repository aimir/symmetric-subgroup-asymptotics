# Two affine-nine blocks: structural certificate

Version 1 is a gzip-compressed JSON object with `kind: affine9_structure`,
`schema_version`, `subgroups`, `locals` and `counts`. The
[dataset](../data/affine9_structure.json.gz) and
[checker](../../computations/gap/affine9_structure.g) give constructive coverage
of actual transitive groups on eighteen points having a two-block system whose
induced primitive local action is affine of degree nine.

The result identifies these groups with exactly 98 conjugacy classes in S18,
represented by the shared [literal action alphabet](../data/nonbinary_affine9_actions.json).
It does not classify nonaffine primitive local actions. The separate normal
and character certificates on that same alphabet supply the counting inputs;
this package supplies their structural coverage.

## Literal conventions

Permutations are one-based image lists, using GAP's right-action convention.
Every subgroup retains generators in its actual ambient permutation action.
Conjugators are actual permutations, not abstract group isomorphisms. The
identity subgroup has an explicit identity generator. Generator-image lists
follow the source group's reconstructed `GeneratorsOfGroup` order, which is
also the supplied generator order used to construct that source.

`target` in an adjoining edge indexes the matrix subgroup list. In an extension
it is an `18A` identifier in the shared action alphabet. `extension` indexes the
extension list of that particular graph. These three index types have distinct
scopes. The shared identifiers are construction identifiers, not transitive
catalogue indices.

## Natural local actions

The checker constructs all 48 matrices of GL(2,3) in their faithful action on
the nine vectors, the natural translation subgroup, and the four vector-space
lines. Each of the sixteen `subgroups` records contains literal generators,
an `irreducible` flag and an `edges` list. Each edge retains an actual matrix
`element`, a target subgroup index and a GL(2,3) `conjugator` carrying the group
generated by that element and the source subgroup to its target.

All 48 elements must occur once in every edge list. The checker also verifies
the trivial subgroup is present, every representative lies in GL(2,3), and
the sixteen representatives are pairwise nonconjugate there. These conditions
prove coverage: starting from the trivial subgroup, adjoining generators of
any subgroup stays in the represented conjugacy classes. The recorded
conjugators make the induction actual. Irreducibility is tested directly
against all four lines. Exactly seven stabilizers survive.

Each `locals` record identifies its `stabilizer`, retains the exact affine
generators, and carries its `normals` list. The checker reconstructs L as the
natural translations extended by that stabilizer, and checks its primitivity.
The seven local orders are 36,72,72,72,144,216,432. No transitive or primitive
action catalogue is queried.

## Goursat graphs and actual extensions

For each literal normal N of L, the checker forms the actual quotient map
π:L→Q=L/N, its automorphism group A, and I=Inn(Q). The normal record retains
generators and `graphs`. Its complete normal list is compared with a fresh GAP
`NormalSubgroups(L)` computation. This local normal-list completeness uses GAP's
normal-subgroup algorithm; it is separate from the normal-lattice certificate
for each of the 98 degree-eighteen actions.

A graph record has:

* `phi_lifts`: actual elements of L lifting the images under an automorphism φ
  of the quotient images of the supplied generators of L;
* `automorphism_order`: the exact order of φ;
* `kernel`: the literal full Goursat subgroup
  K={(x,y)∈L×L:π(y)=φ(π(x))};
* `extensions` and `involutions`, described below.

The checker reconstructs φ as a bijective quotient endomorphism, checks
φ²∈I, and requires exactly one representative from every right I-coset in A
having that property. There are 75 such graph rows. Seven representatives have
order greater than two: replacing the square-inner condition by φ²=1 would
lose required rows.

Let W=L wr C2 on the actual two blocks, B=L×L, D=N_W(K), P=D/K and
P₀=(D∩B)/K. The checker enumerates every element of order two in P outside P₀.
Each `involutions` record retains an actual `lift` in D, an `extension` index,
and a literal W-conjugator carrying its full preimage to the chosen extension.
The lifts must give precisely all these quotient involutions, without repeats.
There are 141 such elements across the graph rows.

Each extension has its actual `generators`, a shared action `target`, an S18
`conjugator`, and a `nonsplit` flag. The checker verifies U≤W is transitive,
U∩B=K, |U:K|=2, both local projections of K are full L, and the intersection
with the two natural translation groups has order 9 or 81. The extension
representatives within a graph are pairwise nonconjugate in W, and every one
is reached by a retained quotient involution. The actual S18 conjugator must
identify U with its shared target, with every one of the 98 targets reached.
The shared targets are separately checked pairwise nonconjugate in S18.

The `nonsplit` flag means U has no involution outside its specified K. It is
checked on actual conjugacy classes of U. Of 116 graph/extension certificates,
36 have this flag. This number counts certificates with their specified block
kernels; it is not a count of distinct abstract groups. No involutory block
swap is assumed.

## Why this covers the stated actions

For an arbitrary group in scope, its actual block swap identifies the two
local actions and embeds the group in W. Its block kernel projects onto both
copies of L. Goursat's lemma supplies normal kernels N₁,N₂ and a quotient
isomorphism. Conjugation by the actual swap exchanges N₁ and N₂ up to inner
automorphisms of L; since they are normal, N₁=N₂=N. The induced quotient
automorphism has square inner. Conjugating one block by an element of L
allows selection of the retained inner coset representative without changing
the actual S18 conjugacy class.

The whole group normalizes this K, contains it with index two, and is outside
the base. It is therefore exactly the full preimage of one of the retained
nonbase quotient involutions in D/K. The literal W and S18 conjugators then
identify it with a retained target. Conversely, every retained target is
reached by an extension verified to satisfy the stated scope. Thus the
coverage is constructive and does not depend on a transitive action census.

Different graph certificates may reach the same actual target. This is a
coverage list, not a counting multiplicity: it introduces no scalar, extra
ownership, or normalizer divisor. Transporting a normal subgroup or character
to the shared alphabet uses the actual S18 conjugator and the original action.

## Replay boundary

The complete replay checks 16 matrix classes, 768 adjoining edges, seven local
actions, 75 graphs, 141 quotient involutions, 116 extension certificates,
36 nonsplit certificates and all 98 target classes. It performs 5,179 assertions.
Eight altered-certificate controls are rejected by semantic checks.

The computational boundary includes GAP's exact group, normal-subgroup,
automorphism, normalizer, conjugacy and quotient algorithms. This is an
independent replay of supplied literal witnesses, not a reimplementation of
those algorithms or a Lean proof. The Goursat and index-two preimage arguments
above explain how the finite checks imply coverage. The global character
bounds and weighted subgroup asymptotic remain separate mathematical results.
